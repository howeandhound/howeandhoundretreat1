About Me
Hi, I’m Alex.



Howe & Hound started because I struggled to find somewhere I truly trusted with my own dogs.



All three of mine are rescues, and each came to me with their own story. Bandit was pulled from Tennessee after being found in a culvert in a near-death situation. Experiences like his are what gave me such a deep love for rescue and for the dogs who sometimes need a little extra patience and understanding.



When I traveled, I didn’t want to simply drop my dogs off at a kennel. I wanted someone who would get to know them, understand their quirks, give them plenty of time outside, and care for them like their own.



So I created that place.



Howe & Hound is a small, personal boarding experience on 11 beautiful acres, built around giving every dog the attention, space, and care I would want for my own.



This is way deeper than your typical boarding experience, though. Every stay helps me help a dog like Bandit get a second chance at life, and that’s really what Howe & Hound is all about.
